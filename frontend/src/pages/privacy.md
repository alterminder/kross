# Privacy notice

_Last updated: [DATE]_

KROSS ([YOUR DOMAIN]) is a training simulator for frontline humanitarian negotiation. This notice explains what personal
data KROSS processes, why, who receives it, and what you can do about it.

## Who is responsible

KROSS is run by [OPERATOR NAME], who is responsible for your personal data ("we" below).
Contact for anything about privacy: [CONTACT EMAIL].

## Please do not enter real sensitive information

The scenarios are fictional. Do not put real names, real locations of operations, security details, or other
sensitive information about yourself or anyone else into session messages, notes, questions to the coach, briefs for
AI scenario writing, or trainer twists. Session dialogue, coach questions, AI scenario briefs and trainer twists are
sent to an AI model provider in China (see below), and so are preparation and reflection notes when the coach uses
them. We do not remove identifying details from that text. Trainer comments on turns and private observer notes are
not sent to the AI.

## What we process

**If you sign in with Google:** we request Google's basic profile and email scopes. We store your Google account ID,
your name, your email address and whether Google verified it. Other basic profile fields Google may return (such as a
picture) are not saved. We do not request access to your contacts, files, calendar or other Google data.

**If you play as a guest:** no name or email. We create a random guest account.

**What you write and what the simulator produces:** your messages in a session, your preparation and reflection
notes, questions you ask the debrief coach, briefs and scenarios you write (including your answers in the guided
scenario builder), the simulator's replies, scores and debriefs, and the pictures painted for scenarios you build,
which are stored with the scenario as its cover.

**In an organisation:** your membership and role. Trainer comments on your turns and trainer twists are stored with
the session.

**Usage records:** for each AI model call, the number of input and output tokens (pieces of text), the time, the model
and its role, whether the call succeeded and how long it took, and the random identifiers of the account,
organisation and session it belonged to. These records contain no message text. We use them to apply daily limits
and control costs.

We also keep counters for session starts, write requests and allocated storage, and reservations for paid AI work.
They contain random account and organisation identifiers and counts, not message text. Deleting content does not
reset these allowances. Obsolete daily write counters are removed during the daily clean-up; cumulative storage
counters remain to prevent repeated deletion from bypassing limits.

**Technical data:** our servers log requests, including your IP address, to keep the service running and secure.

**If you write to us:** the contact details and whatever you include, to answer your request.

## Why, and on what legal basis

- **To provide the training service you asked for** (accounts, playing scenarios, debriefs, organisations, writing
  scenarios): performance of our agreement with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
- **To keep the service secure and affordable** (sign-in sessions, server logs, abuse prevention, daily usage limits):
  our legitimate interest in running a safe service that we can pay for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not track you across other sites.

## Who receives your data

- **Admins of organisations you join (your trainers):** your name, email address and role. For solo sessions you
  play while that organisation is selected, they can read the session, your preparation, reflection and debrief,
  follow it live, and add comments that you do not see. Test runs of your own scenarios (playtests) are visible only
  to you.
- **Other people in KROSS, for scenarios you share:** members of an organisation can read scenarios shared with it;
  its admins can read private drafts you submit to them for publication; public scenarios can be read by anyone
  using KROSS, including guests, and are managed by the people who run KROSS.
- **Our hosting provider** in the European Union, which runs our servers and database.
- **Our AI model provider, DeepSeek** (People's Republic of China). It receives the text used for simulation and
  feedback: the relevant scenario content, the dialogue and the simulator's replies, scores and outcomes, trainer
  twists, questions to the coach (each is first sent for a topic check, even if it is then refused) and recent coach
  chat, your preparation and reflection notes when the coach uses them, and briefs and builder answers you submit
  for AI scenario writing (even if you never save or play the result). We do not attach your account name, email address or Google
  account ID to these requests, but if you type identifying information into that text, DeepSeek receives it.
- **Google**, to sign you in.
- **Google's Gemini API** (Google LLC, United States), to paint scenario pictures in the guided builder. It receives
  a short scene description that our AI model writes from your scenario text (places, vehicles, situation; the model
  is told to leave out names),
  and, for the final picture, the first picture to update. Google LLC takes part in the EU-U.S. and Swiss-U.S. Data
  Privacy Frameworks.
- **The people who run KROSS**, when needed for support, security or usage limits.

## Transfer to China

Text sent to the AI model is processed by DeepSeek on servers in the People's Republic of China. Neither the European
Union nor Switzerland considers China to offer an adequate level of data protection, and we do not have a data
protection agreement or standard contractual clauses with DeepSeek. This means that text may be accessible to Chinese
authorities under Chinese law, and you may not be able to enforce your rights against DeepSeek. This is why you should
keep real sensitive information out of it. If you do not accept this transfer, please do not use the AI features.

## How long we keep data

These periods apply to KROSS's own database. Deleting data in KROSS does not delete copies DeepSeek or Google may
already have kept of text or pictures sent to them. A scenario's cover picture is kept and deleted together with the
scenario, so the cover of a shared scenario stays with it.

- **Guests:** a guest's sign-in lasts at most 24 hours. Guest accounts and their sessions are removed by a daily
  clean-up once the account was created before the previous day (UTC), normally within three days.
- **Play sessions of signed-in users:** removed by a daily clean-up about 12 months after they end. A session that was
  never finished is removed about 12 months after its last turn or turn attempt. Viewing a session or editing its
  notes does not extend this. Sessions are also removed earlier when you delete your account.
- **Your account:** until you delete it, which you can do yourself under _My account_. If you are the only admin of an
  organisation that has other members, first make another member an admin. Deleting your account removes your
  sessions, debriefs and private scenarios, and organisations where you are the only member.
- **Scenarios you shared:** scenarios shared with an organisation that remains, and public scenarios, stay after you
  delete your account. The link to your account is removed, but names or other personal details written into a
  scenario's text are not erased automatically.
- **Usage records and cumulative abuse-prevention counters:** kept without an automatic end date. Deleting your account removes the account they point to, but
  not the records or their identifiers.
- **Server logs:** only as long as needed to run and secure the service.

## Cookies and browser storage

We use only cookies that are strictly necessary: one that keeps you signed in (`nlab_session`, up to 7 days, or 24
hours for guests) and one that protects the Google sign-in for 10 minutes (`nlab_oauth`). Your browser also keeps, for
the current tab session, a few interface choices and an invitation link's token while you sign in (removed once used).
A trainer's private observer notes stay in that browser until they are erased or the site's browser data is cleared;
signing out or deleting the account does not remove them. The sign-in cookies are sent to KROSS to recognise you, and
an invitation token is sent to KROSS to show and accept the invitation; observer notes and interface choices are never
uploaded. There are no analytics or advertising cookies, so there is nothing to consent to.

## Automated feedback

The AI scores your negotiation turns and writes a debrief. This is training feedback only. KROSS makes no decisions
about you with legal or similarly significant effects; if your organisation looks at your scores, a person decides
what to do with them.

## Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, a copy in a portable format, correction, deletion, restriction of processing,
and you can object to processing based on our legitimate interests. Write to the contact address above; we answer
within 30 days. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and
Information Commissioner (FDPIC), in the EU the authority of the country where you live or work.

## Google user data

KROSS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including
the Limited Use requirements. We use your Google account ID, name and email address only to sign you in and show who
you are inside KROSS.

## Children

KROSS is meant for adults in humanitarian work. It is not intended for anyone under 16.

## Changes

If we change this notice, we update the date at the top. If a change matters for how your data is used, we will tell
signed-in users in the app.
